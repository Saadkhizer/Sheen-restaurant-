## Brand character

The naming is the personality. Menu items are puns and in-jokes rather than
descriptors — "Formal Sheen," "AF Sheen," "KF Sheen," "Shash-O-Panj,"
"Teen Batta Sheen," "Double Sawari," "Small-A." Urdu/Persian wordplay mixed
with internet slang. Prices are deliberately repeating digits (333, 888, 999,
1222, 5555).

This matters for the site: the tone should be confident and playful, not the
generic warm-earthy-Mediterranean template every shawarma site defaults to.
The menu copy is already doing the work — the design should get out of its way.

Observed on the physical menu boards: bold red/orange on cream, heavy display
type, illustrated food. "The sweet side of Sheen" appears as the cookie
section header.

## What reviews suggest about positioning

Recurring praise centres on the house bread, the hummus, portion size, and
freshness — several reviewers rank it above other Islamabad shawarma options.
Recurring criticism is about price relative to expectation and occasional
inconsistency in fries texture and sauce quantity. Multiple customers have
asked for lamb.

**Site implication:** price is the main objection, so the site should justify
it visually — house-made bread, hummus, and sauces are the differentiators
worth leading with, not a generic "authentic taste" claim.
